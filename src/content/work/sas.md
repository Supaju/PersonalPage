## Problem
Early-stage products need to reach market quickly, and the skills they need change from one product to the next.

## Approach
I'm brought in as a flexible full-stack developer. For each product I pick up the skills it needs and lean on them to find quick turnarounds, rather than waiting on a perfect fit.

## Impact
Early products reached market faster with a developer who could adapt to whatever the team needed. I've done this since joining SAS as an intern in 2018.
